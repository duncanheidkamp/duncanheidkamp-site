Then listen again

Ten tracks you know inside out will teach you more than a hundred you've heard once.

Pick one recording from today and give it five passes: once for the whole thing, once following only the drums, once only the bass, once only one soloist, and once counting the form. Come back to it a week later without notes.

And go hear it live. A recording is a snapshot of one night; in a room you can watch the drummer cue the band.
