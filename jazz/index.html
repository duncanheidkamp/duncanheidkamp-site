Duncan Heidkamp

Ears Wide Open

A slow walk through jazz in five galleries. Read the wall, press play, and look at the painting while the music does the teaching.

Headphones help. Once a recording starts, the label fades and it's just you, the painting and the band. There are 33 recordings and no finish line; the page remembers where you stopped.

Enter the first gallery

Based on the Ears Wide Open field guide (PDF), after Ted Gioia's How to Listen to Jazz and The Honest Broker. Recordings play from official YouTube uploads. Paintings are public-domain works from The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

Then listen again

Ten recordings you know inside out will teach you more than a hundred you've heard once.

Pick one track from today and give it five passes: once for the whole thing, once following only the drums, once only the bass, once only one soloist, and once counting the form. Come back to it a week later without notes.

And go hear it live. A recording is a snapshot of one night; in a room you can watch the drummer cue the band.
